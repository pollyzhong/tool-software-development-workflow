# Project Intake

Use the smallest set of questions that resolves high-cost ambiguity.

## Questions

1. Who is the primary user, and what result must they achieve?
2. Which platforms are mandatory: browser, desktop, mobile, or all?
3. What is the smallest complete workflow from entry to saved/shared result?
4. Which three capabilities are non-negotiable?
5. What may be delayed without weakening the core promise?
6. Must the product work offline, save large files, collaborate, or share links?
7. Are there reference products, screenshots, existing code, or required technologies?
8. What are the time, budget, privacy, and hosting constraints?
