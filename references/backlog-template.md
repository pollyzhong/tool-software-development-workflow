# Backlog Template

Use this structure for `BACKLOG.md`.

```markdown
# Backlog

## Active Batch

- Goal:
- Included:
- Excluded:
- Acceptance criteria:

## Must Have

| Item | User outcome | Dependencies | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Next

| Item | Value | Cost/risk | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Later

| Idea | Why deferred | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Decisions Needed

- Decision, options, and deadline

## Discovered During Development

- Date: idea or issue; keep out of the active batch unless blocking
```

Keep entries outcome-based. Do not turn every implementation detail into a product item.
